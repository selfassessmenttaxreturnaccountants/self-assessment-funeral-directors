Self Assessment for Funeral Directors: Funeral Plans, Family Fees and the Cars

A self-employed funeral director's year is a run of arrangements: some paid by families at the time, some released from funeral plans taken out years before. Self Assessment is where the year's arrangements are added together.

Arrangement income

Your fee for each funeral is income when the funeral is carried out. Disbursements you collect and pass on - crematorium, cemetery and doctors' fees - are the family's money through your hands, not your income. Plan funerals pay out through the provider: the amount released for your arrangement work is the income, in the year the funeral happens, not when the plan was sold.

The cars and the kit

Hearse and limousine running costs - fuel, insurance, maintenance, cleaning - are the trade's costs, claimed by one consistent method if you also use a car privately. Coffins and supplies you hold are stock: what is on the shelf at the year end is not yet a cost. Chapel or mortuary rent and any embalming room hire are premises costs of the trade.

Out-of-hours and staff

Removals and out-of-hours call-outs are the same trade's income at the price charged. Bearers and drivers you pay casually need proper records - if they work regular patterns under your direction, their status needs a proper look rather than an assumption.

The deadline

File online and pay by 31 January 2027 for 2025/26. Arrangement records kept through the year turn the return into a summary instead of a reconstruction.
